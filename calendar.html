Your lawn
Tall fescue and fine fescue are both cool-season grasses, so the whole program below runs on the cool-season calendar: the big pushes are in fall (aeration, seeding, fertilizer), spring gets a light touch, and summer is mostly maintenance + monitoring. Any Kentucky bluegrass mixed in rides along on the same schedule — it just self-repairs bare spots via rhizomes better than the fescues do. Bermuda is the odd one out: it's a warm-season grass that wants the opposite calendar, which is exactly why it invades cool-season lawns in summer and why it needs its own removal track below.

Mowing height by season
Mow high, especially in summer — a taller canopy shades the soil, keeps fescue roots cooler, crowds out crabgrass, and is one of your best tools against Bermuda encroachment. Never remove more than 1/3 of the blade in a single cut.
